01 The House

Everything good here comes out of one pot.

The Crab House has been feeding Hattiesburg Cajun seafood and hot wings out of a plain building on Hardy Street for years — no tablecloths, no reservations, no fuss. You order at the counter, they call your name, and you leave with more food than you planned on.

Everything is prepped fresh daily and seasoned with the same house blend, whether it goes in the pot or the fryer. That is the whole trick, and it has never needed changing.

02 The Boil

From the water to the tray.

Five steps, one seasoning, and a pot that has not been allowed to cool down properly in a very long time.

32°F
Cold Gulf → rolling boil
  1. 01

    It comes in cold

    Gulf shrimp, snow crab clusters, blue crab and crawfish when the season is on. Delivered and prepped the same day it is sold — nothing sits.

    Daily prep
  2. 02

    It gets the blend

    One Cajun seasoning runs the whole kitchen — cayenne, paprika, garlic, black pepper and the parts they will not tell you. Boiled or fried, it is the same blend.

    House recipe
  3. 03

    It goes in the pot

    A hard rolling boil with corn, red potatoes, andouille and whole garlic, then straight into the bag with garlic butter so it keeps working on the way to you.

    212°F
  4. 04

    Or it hits the oil

    Cornmeal-dredged shrimp, catfish, oysters and fish nuggets, dropped to order and served with fries, hush puppies and slaw. Wings from six pieces to a hundred.

    To order
  5. 05

    Then they call your name

    Counter service, no waiting on a table. Order online and it is bagged and ready when you walk in — which is how most of Hattiesburg does it.

    Pickup ready

04 The Story

Hardy Street, seven days a week.

The Crab House Restaurant serves Cajun seafood and hot wings in Hattiesburg, Mississippi. A convenient location and honest prices make it a natural choice for eat-in or take-out — which is exactly what it was built to be.

The kitchen is known for the range of its seafood and for insisting on fresh, high-quality ingredients. Come in for the friendly room and the kind of service that remembers your usual.
